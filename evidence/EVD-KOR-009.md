# EVD-KOR-009 — AI Seoul Summit hosting and the Seoul Declaration (2024)

## Evidence (from Source)

South Korea co-hosted the AI Seoul Summit (21 May 2024), building on the UK's Bletchley Park AI Safety Summit (November 2023). Eleven signatories (Australia, Canada, the EU, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Korea, Singapore, the UK, and the US) adopted the Seoul Declaration for Safe, Innovative and Inclusive AI, affirming cooperation on AI safety, innovation, and inclusivity; supporting the Hiroshima Process International Code of Conduct; welcoming the Seoul Statement of Intent toward International Cooperation on AI Safety Science; and committing to engagement through the UN, G7, G20, OECD, the Council of Europe, and GPAI.
