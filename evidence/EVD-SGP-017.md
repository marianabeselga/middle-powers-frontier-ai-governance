# EVD-SGP-017 — Singapore's AISI designation and co-lead role across the Network's joint testing exercises

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-IMDA-2024-003; SRC-SGAISI-2024-001 (existing, independently verified — first exercise); SRC-UKAISI-2025-001 (existing, independently verified — third exercise)
- **Country/case:** CTY-SGP
- **Episode ID:** EP-AISI-NETWORK-2024
- **Source location:** IMDA Digital Trust Centre AISI-designation factsheet; first and third joint testing exercise reports

## Quotation / paraphrase

Singapore's Digital Trust Centre was designated its AI Safety Institute on 22 May 2024, with a stated intent to "collaborate with other countries' AISIs." This is the strongest founding-membership case among the four study-relevant countries because it is followed by documented, completed, differentiated leadership roles: Singapore (with the US and UK) led the Network's first joint testing exercise (Nov. 2024, Llama-3.1 405B across three benchmarks); Singapore led the strand on sensitive-information leakage and fraud in the Network's third joint testing exercise (Feb.–Jul. 2025), with France, Kenya, Australia, Japan, Korea, and Canada "contribut[ing] tasks, validat[ing] datasets, translat[ing] materials, or ra[nning] test variations" under Singapore's leadership of that strand.

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C4/C5 (capability mobilised — leadership/convening role, already coded at Stage 1 in `EVD-SGP-008`); M1 (coalition-building — organising a multi-country technical strand); M2 (information sharing); M3b (technical coordination); M4 (joint monitoring/evaluation)
- **Evidence strength:** ES1 — Strong (E1/E2, independently verified for the testing-exercise components; the AISI-designation source not independently re-fetched this round, see `SRC-IMDA-2024-003` Notes)
- **Coding status:** RAW
