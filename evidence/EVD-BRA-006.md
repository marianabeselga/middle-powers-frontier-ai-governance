# EVD-BRA-006 — Middle-power positioning, BRICS GGAI Declaration, and G20 Rio Declaration

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-IEP-2026-001; SRC-BRICS-2025-001; SRC-G20-2024-001

## Quotation / paraphrase

**IEP:** Brazil classified as a middle power and Latin American front-runner; average middle-power nominal GDP ~US$950bn; "Japan's nominal GDP is only twice as high as Brazil, the middle power with the highest nominal GDP. The middle power nations of Brazil, Canada, South Korea, and Australia all have a higher nominal GDP than the great power Russia, when measured in constant 2015 terms."

**BRICS GGAI Declaration (4 extracts):** global AI governance should address Global South needs, operate under national regulatory frameworks and the UN Charter, and respect sovereignty; "Safety and Trust must be Inherent to AI Systems"; "Digital Sovereignty and the Right to Development are Central to Global AI Governance"; a prudent approach to AGI research; support for the UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance and the International Independent Scientific Panel on AI.

**G20 Rio Declaration:** commitments on digital inclusion, digital public infrastructure, transparency/explainability, cross-border data flows; Artificial Intelligence §77 — pursuing safe, secure, trustworthy AI, human rights protection, and "a pro-innovation regulatory/governance approach to AI that will limit risks while enabling us to benefit from what it has to offer."
