# EVD-CAN-004 — AI for All: economic/market ecosystem and adoption gap

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-ISED-2026-002 (AI for All)
- **Country/case:** CTY-CAN
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** AI for All, "Outcomes for Canada," "Creating an investment flywheel," "Accelerating SME commercialization," "The State of AI in Canada," "Canada has a major adoption gap"

## Quotation / paraphrase

**Market scale/investment:** "Canada now ranks 5th globally in AI venture capital with $3.1B invested in 2025." Canada's digital sector employs ~800,000 workers and contributes over $140 billion to GDP, with 150,000 jobs directly tied to AI. Over 3,500 firms are "actively developing advanced AI models, tools, and applications, collectively raising more than CAD$37 billion in venture capital funding." Projected effects: up to 250,000 new jobs through AI adoption by 2031; a targeted 3% GDP increase (~$200 billion) from labour productivity gains; a $500M Canadian Tech Growth Fund; $1.75B in Budget 2025 investment/commitments to stimulate VC.

**Constraining (adoption gap):** Statistics Canada reports "only 12 percent of Canadian businesses used AI to produce goods or services between mid-2024 and mid-2025, rising to 14.5 percent planning to do so by mid-2026." Only ~8% of Canadian SMEs have adopted AI, "well behind Nordic leaders (29 to 42 percent), Germany (26 percent), and France (18 percent)." Individual AI diffusion: Canada ranks 15th globally at 37%. KPMG–University of Melbourne trust study: Canada ranked 44th of 47 on AI training/literacy and 42nd of 47 on trust in AI systems.
