# EVD-KOR-015 — Korea's founding-meeting participation and joint testing exercise contribution

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-MSIT-2024-002; SRC-MSIT-2024-001 (existing, founding membership); SRC-UKAISI-2025-001 (existing, independently verified)
- **Country/case:** CTY-KOR
- **Episode ID:** EP-AISI-NETWORK-2024
- **Source location:** MSIT release on Korea's participation in the Network's founding meeting; Subsequent Multilateral Joint Testing Exercises report

## Quotation / paraphrase

At the Network's founding meeting (Nov. 2024), Korea's delegation presented domestic research (deepfake detection; an AI Risk Assessment and Verification Framework) and participated in discussions on the Network's mission, operational framework and technical approaches; MSIT characterises this as having "helped define international research priorities and foster collaboration." This is Korea's own presentation and participation *at* a founding meeting — the source does not document a specific negotiation, exchange, or joint decision with a named foreign counterpart, nor a resulting joint output specifically traceable to Korea's presentation. **The stronger, independently-verified M-relevant fact:** in the third joint testing exercise, "Australia, Japan and Korea validated Singapore's English dataset annotations," and Korea (with Australia, Canada, France, Japan, Kenya) "translated datasets into their respective languages and annotated the results" (SRC-UKAISI-2025-001).

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C4/C5 (capability mobilised — domestic research presented, institutional participation); M2/M4 (joint testing exercise contribution)
- **Evidence strength:** ES2 — Moderate for the founding-meeting presentation (not independently re-fetched, self-characterised by MSIT); ES1 — Strong for the joint testing exercise component (independently verified)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

The founding-meeting presentation is the same evidentiary pattern already identified and downgraded for Korea's Bletchley host-authorship claim: a national government's own retrospective characterisation of having "helped define" collective priorities, without a documented record of the actual negotiation/exchange content. Applying the same standard consistently: **this does not independently establish M for Korea.** Korea's genuinely established M-relevant contribution is its documented, differentiated participation in the third joint testing exercise, treated as part of the Episode-level "Joint Testing Exercises" configuration, not an independent Korea-specific M finding.
