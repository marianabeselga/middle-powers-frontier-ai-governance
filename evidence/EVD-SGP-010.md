# EVD-SGP-010 — UNCTAD Technology and Innovation Report 2025: Singapore ranking

## Evidence (from Source)

Singapore ranks 5th overall in the UNCTAD Technology and Innovation Report 2025's frontier-technology readiness index, with its strongest sub-rankings in Industry (4th) and Skills (5th), followed by ICT (12th) and Finance (11th); its R&D sub-ranking is comparatively weaker (20th).

## Constructs supported

- **C1 — Technical expertise:** comparative index evidence corroborating (not substituting for) the primary-source evidence in [EVD-SGP-001](EVD-SGP-001.md), per Codebook §18/§22 Rule 11. The relatively weak R&D sub-ranking (20th, the weakest sub-dimension among the six cases' UNCTAD profiles reviewed) is preserved rather than smoothed over — it is directly consistent with Singapore's own strategy documents, which explicitly frame Singapore's strength as deployment/adoption/absorption of AI rather than frontier R&D.
