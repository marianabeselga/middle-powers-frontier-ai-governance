# EVD-SGP-009 — Paris AI Action Summit: Global AI Assurance Pilot, Joint Testing Report with Japan, Red Teaming Challenge, and Leaders' Statement endorsement

## Evidence (from Source)

At the AI Action Summit in Paris (10–11 February 2025), Minister Josephine Teo announced three initiatives: the Global AI Assurance Pilot (AI Verify Foundation and IMDA, convening AI assurance/testing vendors with GenAI deployers to shape assurance standards and grow third-party testing markets); a Joint Testing Report with Japan under the AISI Network on multilingual LLM guardrails (10 languages, 5 harm categories); and the Singapore AI Safety Red Teaming Challenge Evaluation Report 2025 (350+ participants from nine Asia-Pacific countries, 4 LLMs including the domestic SEA-LION model, tested for cultural-bias stereotypes in non-English languages). Singapore endorsed the Leaders' Statement on Inclusive and Sustainable Artificial Intelligence for People and the Planet at the Summit's conclusion.

## Constructs supported

- **C4 — Institutional/diplomatic resources:** convening a bilateral testing collaboration with Japan, launching a multi-vendor assurance pilot, and organising a nine-country regional red-teaming challenge together demonstrate sustained convening capacity beyond passive summit attendance, corroborating [EVD-SGP-008](EVD-SGP-008.md).
- **C5 — Evaluation and safety expertise:** the Red Teaming Challenge and Joint Testing Report both represent Singapore-organised, methodologically substantive AI safety evaluation work (cultural-bias/cross-lingual testing methodology, benchmark development).
- **S3 — Geopolitical alignments:** endorsing the Leaders' Statement and engaging bilaterally with Japan and multilaterally at the AI Action Summit (building on Bletchley 2023 and Seoul 2024) supports **S3 — Enabling**.
