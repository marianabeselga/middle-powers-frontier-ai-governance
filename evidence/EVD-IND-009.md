# EVD-IND-009 — G20 Troika Declaration on Digital Public Infrastructure, AI and Data for Governance

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-MEA-2024-001
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** Full declaration text, Rio de Janeiro, 20 November 2024

## Quotation / paraphrase

Joint communiqué by the G20 Troika (India, Brazil, South Africa): calls for "a technology neutral approach to create a level-playing field for market participants," common design principles (open, modular, interoperable, scalable) for digital public infrastructure (DPI), "fair and equitable principles for data governance," and foundation/frontier AI models "trained on diverse and properly representative data sets... cognizant of the diversity of language and culture." Recalls the UN Global Digital Compact and the Cairo Global DPI Summit (2024). Also documents "four consecutive Global South G20 presidencies (Indonesia, India, Brazil, and South Africa)... with achievements including African Union permanent membership and new initiatives on hunger, taxation, and development" (per the accompanying IEP characterisation, [EVD-IND-008](EVD-IND-008.md)).

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C4 (Institutional/diplomatic resources); S3 (Geopolitical alignments)
- **Evidence strength:** ES1 — Strong (E2 primary international/institutional)
- **Coding status:** RAW
