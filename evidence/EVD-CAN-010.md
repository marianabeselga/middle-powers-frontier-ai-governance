"We will make the most of Canada's strong AI research community as well as in-house expertise to conduct leading-edge research in collaboration with industry and the International Network of AI Safety Institutes... We conduct research in two key ways: Applied and investigator-led research [through CIFAR's CAISI Research Program]... Government-directed projects... including joint testing with international counterparts."

As a founding member of the network, CAISI will actively coordinate efforts with international partners and collaborate on joint projects, including the development of guidance on AI safety."
