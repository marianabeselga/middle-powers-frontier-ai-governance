# EVD-JPN-004 — Japan AI Safety Institute (J-AISI): institutional profile and evaluation capacity

## Evidence (from Source)

J-AISI coordinates across roughly 12 ministries/agencies, with its secretariat hosted by IPA under METI/Digital Agency. Its published outputs include an AI safety evaluation/red-teaming guide, data-quality guidance, and a Chief AI Officer Guidebook. It maintains a crosswalk with the US NIST AI framework and is characterised in the Research Note as exercising a form of "regulatory soft power." J-AISI was established in February 2024; its National Status Report on AI Safety in Japan 2024 (published February 2025) documents these activities.

## Constructs supported

- **C4 — Institutional/diplomatic resources:** cross-ministerial institutional coordination (12 ministries/agencies) and an active international technical relationship (NIST crosswalk) support a **Strong** C4 rating, consistent with [EVD-JPN-009](EVD-JPN-009.md) and [EVD-JPN-010](EVD-JPN-010.md).
- **C5 — Evaluation and safety expertise:** the evaluation/red-teaming guide, data-quality guidance, and Chief AI Officer Guidebook, combined with the NIST crosswalk, indicate substantial dedicated technical evaluation/safety capacity — the Research Note characterises this as Japan's strongest capability dimension.
