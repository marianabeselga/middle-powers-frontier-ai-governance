# EVD-CAN-011 — Canada's Bletchley Summit position and Ministerial-level representation (1–3 Nov. 2023)

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-ISED-2023-001; SRC-UKGOV-2023-001

## Quotation / paraphrase

Minister François-Philippe Champagne represented Canada at the Bletchley AI Safety Summit and "led a discussion among governmental leaders from around the globe on risks to safety from frontier AI misuse." He is quoted: "It is critical and urgent that we all come together to build public trust around this transformational technology." The press release states Canada's approach "aligns with G7 Hiroshima Process principles and the U.S. Executive Order on safe AI development." This adds ministerial-representation and position detail to the existing Table 3 record (CTY-CAN / Bletchley Declaration — "represented / participant").
