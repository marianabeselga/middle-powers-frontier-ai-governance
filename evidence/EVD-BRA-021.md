Laercio Aniceto Silva (Centers of Reference in Innovative Technologies, Brazil) and Norberto Ferreira (Center for Research and Development in Telecommunications, Brazil) are confirmed named participants in the GPAI Innovation & Commercialisation Working Group, which has produced recurring reports since 2020.
