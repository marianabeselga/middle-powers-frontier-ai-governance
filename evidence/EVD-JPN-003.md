# EVD-JPN-003 — AI Business Operator Guidelines: soft-law layer on the binding Act

## Evidence (from Source)

The AI事業者ガイドライン (AI Business Operator Guidelines), issued by METI (with MIC involvement), provides detailed non-binding operational guidance for AI developers, providers, and business users, layered on top of the binding AI Promotion Act (see [EVD-JPN-002](EVD-JPN-002.md)). The Research Note characterises Japan's overall regulatory approach as "light statutory regulation combined with detailed soft-law guidance."

## Constructs supported

- **C2 — Regulatory expertise/capacity:** demonstrates regulatory-guidance capacity (detailed operator-level guidelines) but confirms the soft-law, non-enforcement character of the guidance layer — consistent with, and part of the basis for, Japan's overall **C2 — C-2 (Moderate)** rating (see [EVD-JPN-002](EVD-JPN-002.md) for the combined justification).
- **S4 — Regulatory/institutional arrangements:** the guidelines contribute to an enabling, coordinated regulatory/institutional environment, complementing the binding Act.
