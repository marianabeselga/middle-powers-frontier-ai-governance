# EVD-IND-020 — India–Japan Joint Statement on AI Cooperation: bilateral coordination including explicit Hiroshima Friends Group deepening

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-PMINDIA-2026-002 (independently verified in full)
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** EP-HIROSHIMA-AI-2024
- **Source location:** India–Japan Joint Statement on Cooperation in AI, 2 July 2026

## Quotation / paraphrase

A substantive, negotiated bilateral joint statement between PM Modi and PM Takaichi, including named research MOUs signed (IIT Bombay/BharatGen/NII; Sarvam/Preferred Networks; IndiaAI/METI), a recurring "India and Japan AI Strategic Dialogue" (first held April 2026), and, specifically: "They reaffirmed their commitment to deepen cooperation within the Hiroshima AI Process Friends Group and Partners Community, including through greater participation of the Global South and the private sector, and promote the implementation of the Hiroshima AI Process Friends Group Action Plan 2026." The two governments also "decided to strengthen coordination... in international fora, including the G20, OECD, GPAI, and the United Nations."

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C4 (diplomatic capacity, substantively mobilised); M1 (coalition-building — established, joint commitment to deepen the Friends Group specifically); M2 (information sharing — the AI Strategic Dialogue mechanism); M3a/M3c (regulatory/diplomatic coordination — signed MOUs, coordinated positioning across multilateral fora)
- **Evidence strength:** ES1 — Strong (E1, independently fetched and read in full; corroborated by Japan's own Kantei documentation and multiple news outlets)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

This is the strongest coordination-mechanism evidence found for any non-Japan actor in this Episode. Unlike India's Bletchley-episode finding (organisational structuring of a domestic consultative apparatus) or mere Friends Group membership, this is a genuine bilateral negotiated joint statement with concrete deliverables (signed MOUs, an established recurring dialogue mechanism) and an explicit, specific, Hiroshima-targeted commitment — not a general endorsement. **Coded M1/M2/M3a/M3c — established**, for the "India–Japan bilateral AI cooperation" configuration specifically. This does not establish an India-wide, autonomous Friends Group leadership role — it is a bilateral (India-Japan) configuration, distinct from the broader Friends Group's own collective/multilateral character.
