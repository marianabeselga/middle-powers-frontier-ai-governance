# EVD-JPN-015 — Paris participation and Council of Europe AI Convention signing (Feb. 2025)

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-MOFAJP-2025-002; SRC-ELYSEE-2025-001 (existing, Paris Statement); SRC-MOFAJP-2025-003 (context only)

## Quotation / paraphrase

Japan participated in the Paris AI Action Summit and signed the Statement on Inclusive and Sustainable AI, supporting an approach combining safety/security/trustworthiness with innovation, and emphasising human rights, democracy, rule of law, and inclusion of developing countries. At the margins of Paris, Japan also signed the Council of Europe Framework Convention on AI — the Research Note itself explicitly flags this as "contextual to the Summit rather than Summit instruments themselves."
