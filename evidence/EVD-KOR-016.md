## Quotation / paraphrase

Two distinct facts. **(1) The bilateral working-level meeting:** named Korean and Japanese officials met in Tokyo, "exchanged views on national AI policies, cooperation on global AI governance in multilateral fora including... Hiroshima AI Process... and concurred to regularly hold such meetings." This is a real, documented bilateral exchange, but Hiroshima is one of five multilateral fora discussed, not the specific negotiated subject. **(2) The Seoul Declaration's reference to Hiroshima:** the Declaration "acknowledges the Hiroshima AI Process Friends Group" and supports operationalisation of the Hiroshima Code of Conduct — this is an instrument (Seoul, a Table 2 component of `EP-BLETCHLEY-SEQUENCE-2023`) referencing another instrument (Hiroshima), an institutional-lineage note, not documented Korea-specific coordination activity.
