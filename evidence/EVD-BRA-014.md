# EVD-BRA-014 — Brazil absent from the Seoul Declaration's Leaders' endorsement (21 May 2024) — Seoul Ministerial Statement not affected

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-MOFA-2024-001; SRC-CARNEGIE-2026-001
- **Country/case:** CTY-BRA
- **Episode ID:** EP-BLETCHLEY-SEQUENCE-2023
- **Source location:** Carnegie Endowment, "For People, Planet, and Progress" (Apr. 2026), Table 1 ("How AI Safety has Featured Through the Summits"): "India, China, Russia, South Africa, and Brazil were notable omissions" from the 2024 AI Seoul Summit endorsements.

## Quotation / paraphrase

Brazil was not among the 11 governments endorsing the Seoul Declaration for Safe, Innovative and Inclusive AI (the Leaders' Declaration) at the May 2024 AI Seoul Summit. This is independently corroborated by two sources: the existing Coordination Universe Matrix Table 3 record (CTY-BRA / Seoul Declaration — "not Leaders' Declaration participant," sourced to SRC-MOFA-2024-001) and the Carnegie Endowment compendium, which lists Brazil among the "notable omissions" from the 2024 Seoul endorsements.

**Scope correction (important):** the researcher's Stage 4 Research Note characterized this non-endorsement as extending to both "the Seoul Declaration or the Seoul Ministerial Statement." The Carnegie source discusses only the Seoul Declaration and makes no claim about the separate Seoul Ministerial Statement. The existing Coordination Universe Matrix Table 3 record for CTY-BRA / Seoul Ministerial Statement (sourced to SRC-UKGOV-2024-002) already documents Brazil as a "ministerial participant" — this is a different instrument, not a contradiction, and this Evidence record does **not** extend the non-endorsement finding to it. No change was made to the existing Table 3 record.

- **Evidence strength:** ES1 — Strong (corroborated across two independent sources: E2 Coordination Universe Matrix basis + E5 Carnegie compendium)
