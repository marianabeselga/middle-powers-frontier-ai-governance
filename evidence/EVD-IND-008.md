## Quotation / paraphrase

"A distinct band of great powers stretches across Eurasia and into Europe, including Russia, India, and several European nations... India's large population base provide natural foundations for great power status... India is the sole great power nation with strong projected economic growth. India has surpassed China as the world's most populous nation and is projected to become the third-largest economy before 2030. No great power nations other than India are projected to record a single year of growth above 2.5 per cent before the end of the decade."

## Researcher notes

**Classification note (flagged for researcher awareness, not blocking Stage 1 coding):** this same IEP report classifies Brazil and Canada as "middle powers" but classifies India as a **"great power"** — a different tier, not a contradiction (the report applies distinct classifications to different countries). Given this project's framing as a study of "middle-power coordination," this is worth the researcher's attention as a case-selection/framing matter, but it does not block Stage 1: India's C4/S3 assessment here proceeds from its documented institutional/diplomatic capabilities and international positioning, independent of which power-tier label the IEP report applies.
