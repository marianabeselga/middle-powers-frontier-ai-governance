# EVD-SGP-007 — International Network of AI Safety Institutes: founding membership

## Evidence (from Source)

Singapore is a founding member of the International Network of AI Safety Institutes, launched 21 November 2024 in San Francisco alongside Australia, Canada, the European Commission, France, Japan, Kenya, the Republic of Korea, the UK, and the US, building on the Seoul Statement of Intent toward International Cooperation on AI Safety Science (AI Seoul Summit, May 2024). The Network's mission covers research, testing, guidance, and inclusion on AI safety.

## Constructs supported

- **C4 — Institutional/diplomatic resources:** founding membership in a major international AI-safety network is evidence of institutional access and engagement — coded strictly as C4 institutional capacity per Codebook §8, not as coordination-mechanism (M) or governance-leverage (GL) evidence per the §11 Critical rule (mere membership is not sufficient evidence of coordination).
- **C5 — Evaluation and safety expertise:** membership presupposes an operating national AI safety institute with a technical mandate; substantiated in practice by the leadership role documented in [EVD-SGP-008](EVD-SGP-008.md).
