# EVD-JPN-018 — Japan's creation, convening, and expansion of the Hiroshima AI Process Friends Group

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-SOUMU-2024-002 (existing, Friends Group); SRC-SOUMU-2026-001 (existing, Action Plan 2026); SRC-GOJ-2023-001 (existing, G7 Leaders' Statement)
- **Country/case:** CTY-JPN
- **Episode ID:** EP-HIROSHIMA-AI-2024
- **Source location:** Friends Group launch materials; second in-person meeting outcome; Action Plan 2026

## Quotation / paraphrase

Japan launched the Hiroshima AI Process during its 2023 G7 Presidency, then launched the Friends Group in May 2024 under its own leadership as "a voluntary framework for countries and regions supporting the spirit of HAIP," with an initial membership of 49 countries/regions. Japan convened a second in-person meeting (Tokyo, March 2026) bringing together 66 countries/regions, private AI companies, and international organizations, producing the "Hiroshima AI Process Friends Group Action Plan 2026" with specific voluntary actions.

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C4 (diplomatic/convening capacity, substantively mobilised); C2 (regulatory/institutional capacity, translating G7 commitments into instruments); M1 (coalition-building — established)
- **Evidence strength:** ES1 — Strong (E1, existing independently-screened Table 2 sources)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

This is Japan's own act of constructing and expanding a coalition — the defining activity of M1 ("processes through which actors construct, expand, maintain, or mobilise a coalition around a governance objective"). This is qualitatively different from a country merely joining an existing coalition (the Critical Rule's excluded categories), and different from Korea's Bletchley-episode host-authorship finding (which was downgraded because it rested on self-characterisation of an already-negotiated collective text): here, Japan's own convening actions produced a directly-observable, growing, structured institution (documented membership growth 49→66; repeated in-person meetings; a specific, dated Action Plan) — this is the coalition-building process itself, not a retrospective claim about influence within someone else's process. **Coded M1 — established**, for the "Friends Group creation and expansion" configuration, with Japan as principal architect. This does not, by itself, establish M for the other four cases (Canada, India, Korea, Singapore) as members — see their individual records.
