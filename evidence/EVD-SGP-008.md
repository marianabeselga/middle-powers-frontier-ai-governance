# EVD-SGP-008 — Third joint testing exercise: Singapore leading the agentic-testing strand on sensitive-information leakage and fraud

## Evidence (from Source)

In the International Network of AI Safety Institutes' third joint testing exercise (following exercises in November 2024 and February 2025), Singapore's AI Safety Institute **led** the strand on leakage of sensitive information and fraud (the UK AISI led the parallel cybersecurity strand). Nine languages and ~1,500 tasks were tested; France, Kenya, Australia, Japan, Korea, and Canada contributed tasks, validated datasets, translated materials, or ran test variations under Singapore's leadership of this strand. The exercise was the largest the Network had run to date.

## Constructs supported

- **C4 — Institutional/diplomatic resources:** leading (not merely participating in) a multi-country technical-testing strand — coordinating contributions from six other countries on task design, translation, and evaluation — is direct evidence of convening and brokering capacity within an international technical body, coded as C4 institutional/diplomatic capability per Codebook §8 (not as an M coordination-mechanism or GL governance-leverage claim per the §11 Critical rule, since this is a capability assessment of Singapore's own convening capacity, not a claim about the Network's coordination properties). This evidence directly answers the qualification the Research Note itself attached to an initial "Moderate/Strong" C4 rating — that further evidence of Singapore "actually brokering coalitions" would be needed to justify a Strong rating — supporting the integration of C4 to **C-3 (Strong)**, documented here as an explicit researcher-style judgment call open to override, not a silent upgrade.
- **C5 — Evaluation and safety expertise:** leading a technically substantial cross-lingual agentic-safety testing strand (methodology design, evaluation criteria, judge-LLM assessment) demonstrates concrete, specialised evaluation capacity beyond the general assurance-toolkit evidence in [EVD-SGP-003](EVD-SGP-003.md).
