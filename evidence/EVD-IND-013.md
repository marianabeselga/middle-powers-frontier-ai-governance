## Quotation / paraphrase

Minister of State Rajeev Chandrasekhar addressed the Bletchley Summit's inaugural plenary on India's behalf, framing India's approach around safety, trust, and accountability, and articulating (via a quoted statement attributed to PM Modi) a normative preference for governance "driven by a coalition of nations rather than just one or two countries." This adds ministerial-representation and position detail to the existing Table 3 record (CTY-IND / Bletchley Declaration — "represented / participant").

## Researcher notes

**Important coding distinction:** the "coalition of nations" language is a **normative position statement** — an argument about how technology governance *should* be structured — not documentation of an actual coalition-formation process, negotiation, or joint activity involving India. It is coded here as P1 only. It is explicitly **not** treated as M1 (coalition-building) or any other coordination-mechanism code: stating a preference for multilateral governance is not itself evidence of participating in one.
