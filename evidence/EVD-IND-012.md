# EVD-IND-012 — Private AI investment and domestic AI ecosystem scale

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-AIINDEX-2026-001; SRC-PIB-2025-001
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** 2026 AI Index Report (Economy); "Transforming India with AI" (PIB, 2025)

## Quotation / paraphrase

Private AI investment, 2025: India US$4.09bn; 108 newly funded AI companies (Stanford 2026 AI Index — consistent with the same table's Canada figures already recorded in [EVD-CAN-007](EVD-CAN-007.md)).

"Transforming India with AI" (PIB, 2025): India's tech sector revenue projected to cross "USD 280 billion"; "over 6 million people are employed in the tech and AI ecosystem"; "1,800+ Global Capability Centres, including more than 500 focused on AI"; "around 1.8 lakh startups," with "nearly 89%" of new startups launched last year using AI; NASSCOM AI Adoption Index score of 2.45/4, with "87% of enterprises actively using AI solutions"; leading adoption sectors (industrial/automotive, consumer goods/retail, BFSI, healthcare) contributing "around 60 percent" of AI's total value; "about 26%" of Indian companies at AI maturity at scale (BCG survey); AI projected to add "$1.7 trillion" to India's economy by 2035.
