# EVD-BRA-011 — Brazil's digital-market scale and deployment ecosystem

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-TRADE-2025-001 (internet-use/e-commerce data, IBGE-attributed); SRC-TBI-2026-001 (comparative strategic-capacity framing); SRC-OECD-2026-001 (Digital Government Outlook, documented as context only — see below)
- **Country/case:** CTY-BRA
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** trade.gov Brazil E-Commerce guide; TBI "Sovereignty in the Age of AI" report; OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026: Brazil

## Quotation / paraphrase

"168.7 million people (90.5% of the population aged 10+) used the Internet, while 95% of Brazilian households had Internet access... 52.7% of Internet users bought or ordered goods/services online, 74.2% used Internet banking, and 41.1% used public services online" (IBGE PNAD Contínua data, as reported via trade.gov).

TBI comparative framing: "India, Kenya and Brazil can derive strategic AI capacity from the scale and quality of their domestic digital ecosystems even where they remain dependent on external technological infrastructure... market and deployment capacity can constitute a distinct strategic resource from technological self-sufficiency, with countries potentially retaining agency despite dependencies in other layers of the AI stack."

**Documented as context, not coded:** OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026 reports Brazil scoring 0.79 on its Digital Government Index (OECD average 0.70), above average on all six dimensions. This measures government digital-service capacity, not market size/deployment ecosystem per Codebook §8's C3 empirical basis, and the researcher's own C3 rationale for this note does not cite it — retained here as documented background only.
