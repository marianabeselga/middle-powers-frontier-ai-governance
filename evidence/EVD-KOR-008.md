# EVD-KOR-008 — AI Safety Institute launch: International Network founding membership and Korea AI Safety Consortium

## Evidence (from Source)

MSIT launched Korea's AI Safety Institute on 27 November 2024, following commitments made at the AI Seoul Summit (May 2024). The institute is a founding member of the International Network of AI Safety Institutes (launched 21 November 2024, 10 countries — US, UK, Japan, Singapore, Canada, and others). Its mission covers AI-risk definition/analysis, safety-policy research, safety-evaluation-criteria/methods research, safety-technology/standardisation research, and international cooperation. A Memorandum of Understanding formed the 24-member Korea AI Safety Consortium (industry, academia, research organisations) to work alongside the institute on risk identification/evaluation/mitigation, policy research, and technological collaboration. The institute's inaugural director explicitly characterised it as "not a regulatory body but a collaborative organization."

## Constructs supported

- **C4 — Institutional/diplomatic resources:** founding membership in the International Network of AI Safety Institutes, and convening/participation alongside the US, UK, and Japan AI Safety Institute directors at the launch ceremony, is evidence of institutional access and international engagement — coded strictly as C4 institutional capacity per Codebook §8, not as coordination-mechanism (M) or governance-leverage (GL) evidence per the §11 Critical rule (mere participation/membership is not sufficient evidence of coordination).
- **C5 — Evaluation and safety expertise:** the institute's substantive mandate (risk analysis, safety-evaluation-criteria research, standardisation) and the 24-member consortium's technical scope provide direct evidence of dedicated evaluation/safety capacity, consistent with the statutory basis documented in [EVD-KOR-003](EVD-KOR-003.md).
