# EVD-JPN-007 — Japan's weak venture-capital ecosystem (OECD benchmarking)

## Evidence (from Source)

The OECD Economic Surveys: Japan 2026 reports Japan's venture capital investment at approximately 0.05% of GDP versus an OECD average of 0.11%; VC investment per capita of roughly US$14 versus an OECD average of US$87; VC market growth of about 5%/year versus an OECD average of 21%/year; and a median deal size of roughly US$2M versus an OECD average of US$4.5M.

## Constructs supported

- **S2 — Market size/dependence:** the OECD benchmarking data documents a structurally weak private-risk-capital ecosystem relative to OECD peers, a constraining factor that qualifies the market-scale strength documented in [EVD-JPN-006](EVD-JPN-006.md) (C3) — together these support an **S2 — Mixed** rating (large/growing product market and industrial base, but comparatively weak venture-financing ecosystem for AI-specific scale-ups).
