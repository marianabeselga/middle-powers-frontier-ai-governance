# EVD-IND-021 — India's 2024 Lead Chairmanship, GPAI–OECD integration, and expert participation

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-OECDAI-2025-001 (independently verified — RAI WG, India: 2 members); SRC-OECDAI-2024-001 (independently verified — Mausam, IIT, Innovation & Commercialisation WG); SRC-GPAI-2024-001 (New Delhi Declaration, verified to resolve to a real document 2026-09-06)
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** EP-GPAI-2024
- **Source location:** RAI report; Innovation & Commercialisation WG roster; New Delhi Declaration (2024)

## Quotation / paraphrase

India served as GPAI Lead Chair for 2024, and under that chairmanship the New Delhi Summit (3–4 July 2024) announced the GPAI–OECD Integrated Partnership, initially bringing together 44 countries. Independently confirmed: India has named individual expert members in both the Responsible AI Working Group (2 members) and the Innovation & Commercialisation Working Group (Mausam, IIT). The researcher's additional claims (Sustainable Agriculture convening, new thematic proposals) were not independently re-fetched.

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C1 (technical, expert participation); C4 (diplomatic/institutional — substantively mobilised via the chairmanship role, a genuine state-level act distinct from expert nomination); M1 (coalition-building/institutional design — established, via the chairmanship's role in the GPAI–OECD integration); M3a (regulatory/diplomatic coordination)
- **Evidence strength:** ES1 — Strong (E1/E2, independently verified WG membership and the Declaration's existence)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

India's chairmanship is the strongest state-level (not merely expert-nomination-level) contribution documented for India in this Episode — organising the New Delhi Summit and its institutional outcome (the OECD integration) is a direct governmental act, not mediated through independent experts. This is analytically comparable to Japan's and Canada's founding/leadership-tier contributions, and stronger than India's own WG-membership-level participation.
