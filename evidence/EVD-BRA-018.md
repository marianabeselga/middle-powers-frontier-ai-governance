## Quotation / paraphrase

Per the researcher's Stage 4 Research Note, President Lula attended the New Delhi AI Impact Summit and argued for global AI governance, international rules/principles/institutions/coordination mechanisms, protection of human rights and information integrity, regulation of major digital platforms, reduction of inequalities in AI infrastructure and data, greater access to AI resources, opposition to excessive concentration of AI power, digital sovereignty, and AI serving social/economic development. He is quoted (via the source's own title) connecting concentration of algorithms/infrastructure to political "domination" rather than mere innovation, and argued that regulation of big tech is necessary to protect human rights and information integrity.
