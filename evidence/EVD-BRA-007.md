# EVD-BRA-007 — CIAI mission and absence of a dedicated AI Safety Institute

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-CIAI-2025-001; SRC-CEBRI-2025-001
- **Country/case:** CTY-BRA
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** CIAI presentation page; CEBRI/ITS report (full text)

## Quotation / paraphrase

**CIAI:** established with a mission to promote the safe, reliable, and responsible use of artificial intelligence in products and services available to Brazilian society, integrating technological innovation with institutional and social objectives.

**CEBRI/ITS:** identifies the absence of a dedicated Brazilian AI Safety and Opportunities Institute and discusses its potential establishment; Brazil is not currently part of the International Network of AI Safety Institutes.

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C5 (Evaluation and safety expertise)
- **Evidence strength:** ES2 — Moderate (combines E1 primary official mission statement with E5 think-tank gap analysis)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

Researcher's provisional assessment: Limited (C-1). Researcher observation: CIAI provides an identifiable but nascent institutional capability; no dedicated national AI Safety Institute exists yet; civil-society proposals and PL 2338/2023 indicate emerging interest but not established evaluation infrastructure.
