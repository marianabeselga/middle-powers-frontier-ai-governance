# EVD-JPN-011 — METI cloud-supply subsidy program and domestic cloud market share

## Evidence (from Source)

Under the Economic Security Promotion Act, METI approved roughly ¥72.5bn in subsidies to five companies/joint ventures (GMO, Sakura Internet, a RUTILEA/AI Fukushima joint venture, KDDI, and a Highreso/Highreso Kagawa joint venture) to build domestic cloud infrastructure. The Research Note flags that domestic providers hold only around 30% of Japan's cloud market, with the remainder dependent on foreign (primarily US) hyperscalers.

## Constructs supported

- **S1 — Compute/infrastructure:** the subsidy program is a state-level enabling effort toward domestic compute sovereignty, but the underlying ~30% domestic cloud market share indicates substantial foreign dependence — together these support an **S1 — Mixed** rating, alongside [EVD-JPN-012](EVD-JPN-012.md).
