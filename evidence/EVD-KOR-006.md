# EVD-KOR-006 — IEP: South Korea as a rising middle power with expanding diplomatic connectivity

## Evidence (from Source)

The IEP's "The Great Fragmentation" report characterises South Korea as one of the most dynamic "Rising Middle Powers" (alongside Türkiye and the UAE), noting that Korea "has surged past established middle powers like Australia and Canada to occupy a high-bandwidth, high-dependence position that rivals the top European states," reflecting a strategy of "coupling export-led economic growth with a diversified diplomatic portfolio, effectively converting economic weight into structural leverage." The report also notes Korea's higher total military expenditure than several "great power" nations and Korea's dominance in "critical segments of advanced manufacturing," and situates Korea within US-aligned Asian partnerships (Japan, Australia, South Korea, India) functioning as a counterweight in the region.
