# EVD-IND-005 — IndiaAI Safety Institute and Responsible AI projects

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-INDIAAI-2026-001; SRC-MEITY-2026-002
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** IndiaAI Safety Institute establishment press releases; Safe & Trusted AI pillar detail

## Quotation / paraphrase

The IndiaAI Safety Institute (CAISI-equivalent) was "announced in January 2025 to advance science-based and indigenous research on AI safety and governance," operating on a "hub-and-spoke model" with academia, startups, industry, and government. Thirteen Responsible AI projects are underway, including: Saakshya (deepfake detection, IIT Jodhpur & IIT Madras); AI Vishleshak (audio-visual forgery detection); a Real-Time Voice Deepfake Detection System (IIT Kharagpur); bias-mitigation work in medical imaging (NIT Raipur); privacy-preserving/explainable federated learning (IIT Delhi with IIIT Delhi and IIT Dharwad; also DIAT); and machine-unlearning research (IIT Jodhpur). A first Expression-of-Interest round selected 8 projects; a second EoI round covers watermarking/labelling, ethical AI frameworks, AI risk assessment, stress-testing tools, and deepfake detection.
