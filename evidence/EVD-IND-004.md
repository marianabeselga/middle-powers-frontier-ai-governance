# EVD-IND-004 — Semiconductor manufacturing capability and GPU import dependence

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-MEITY-2026-002
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** IndiaAI Mission compute-expansion press release, semiconductor section

## Quotation / paraphrase

**Constraining:** "GPUs are highly advanced equipment and are primarily manufactured in one country."

**Enabling/developing domestic capacity:** the Semicon India Programme has approved 10 semiconductor manufacturing units; "commercial production from 1 unit and pilot production from 3 units has already been started"; Tata Electronics Private Limited (TEPL) is building a fab in Gujarat (₹91,526 crore investment; 110nm–28nm nodes; 50,000 Wafer Starts Per Month capacity). The Design Linked Incentive (DLI) Scheme has approved 24 chip/SoC design projects (14 of which raised VC funding), supported 103 fabless chip-design companies, resulted in 7 chips fabricated of 16 taped out (including a 12nm node at TSMC), and produced 10 patent filings and 140+ reusable semiconductor IP cores. National Supercomputing Mission projects are developing RISC-V-based processors/GPGPUs/accelerators to strengthen indigenous HPC/AI hardware capability.
