# EVD-JPN-002 — AI Promotion Act 2025 (Act No. 53): binding framework law, light-touch approach

## Evidence (from Source)

Act No. 53 of 2025 ("Act on Promotion of Research and Development and Utilization of AI-related Technology"), in force since 2025, establishes the AI Strategic Headquarters chaired by the Prime Minister and requires an AI Basic Plan. Its approach is light-touch relative to the EU AI Act: no mandatory risk classification, conformity assessment, or pre-market approval regime; it relies instead on guidance, cross-ministerial coordination, and periodic review.

## Constructs supported

- **C2 — Regulatory expertise/capacity:** a genuinely **binding law already in force** — distinct in legal status from Brazil's pending PL 2338/2023, Canada's withdrawn AIDA, and India's non-binding governance guidelines. However, its light-touch, coordination-based character (no prescriptive risk-tiering or enforcement regime) means it does not by itself indicate the same kind of prescriptive regulatory capacity as a comprehensive statute would. Combined with [EVD-JPN-003](EVD-JPN-003.md), this supports **C2 — C-2 (Moderate)** for Japan (contrast with Canada's C-3/Strong, which rests on a more developed, enforcement-oriented regulatory apparatus).
- **S4 — Regulatory/institutional arrangements:** the AI Strategic Headquarters and AI Basic Plan mechanism represent an enabling institutional/coordination structure.

## Notes

**Law-in-force distinction (critical):** this is the clearest instance across the six cases so far of a country with a binding, already-enacted horizontal AI statute (as opposed to Brazil's pending bill, Canada's withdrawn bill, or India's soft guidelines). This distinction is preserved explicitly here and in [CTY-JPN.md](../00-Countries/CTY-JPN.md) rather than treated as equivalent to other countries' non-binding instruments.
