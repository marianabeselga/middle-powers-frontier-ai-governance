# EVD-BRA-012 — Global Index on Responsible AI (GIRAI): Brazil

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-GIRAI-2025-001
- **Country/case:** CTY-BRA
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** GIRAI Brazil country page

## Quotation / paraphrase

"The 2026 Global Index on Responsible AI (GIRAI) places Brazil 11th globally, with an overall score of 63.3, making it the highest-ranked country in South and Central America and the highest-ranked upper-middle-income country. Brazil performs particularly strongly in Inclusion and Diversity (72.0) and Labour and Skills (76.3), while also scoring relatively highly in AI Use in Public Services (67.1), Ethics and Sustainability (63.4), and Trust and Safety (61.5). The Index identifies Brazil as a 'Bright Spot,' highlighting the combination of policy frameworks and government-led implementation. In particular, Brazil is among the countries with both a policy and a government-led initiative for AI public procurement, and is identified as having implementation activity in environmental impact, labour protection, and disclosure of government AI systems."

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C2 (Regulatory expertise/capacity); S4 (Regulatory and institutional arrangements)
- **Evidence strength:** ES2 — Moderate (E3 secondary index)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

Researcher's provisional assessment (this note): C2 = C-2 Moderate; S4 = Enabling. Rationale (C2): "responsible-AI policy frameworks with evidence of government-led implementation... GIRAI performance further indicates that responsible-AI governance has moved beyond policy formulation toward implementation. However, these indicators do not by themselves demonstrate strong, comprehensive AI-specific enforcement or administrative capacity across regulatory domains. C2 is therefore assessed as Moderate rather than Strong." This is **convergent** with the existing C2 = Moderate rating (from [EVD-BRA-005](EVD-BRA-005.md)) — additional supporting evidence, no change to the assessed level.

Rationale (S4): "Brazil has established institutional and policy arrangements that facilitate coordination around AI governance... These arrangements support an Enabling assessment for S4. However, they do not by themselves demonstrate mature AI-specific regulatory enforcement capacity... The PBIA itself identifies significant remaining gaps in the practical and strategic governance of AI, which further supports distinguishing institutional enablement from regulatory capacity."

**S4 assessment retained as Mixed, not changed to Enabling (considered judgment, not a mechanical pick):** although this note's own headline label proposes "Enabling," its full reasoning explicitly reaffirms the same constraining fact already underlying Brazil's existing S4 = Mixed rating — "significant remaining gaps in the practical and strategic governance of AI." This is not a fresh, independent conflict between two different metrics (unlike CTY-CAN's C3, where two genuinely distinct indicators pointed different ways); it is the same underlying picture (developed institutional architecture coexisting with an incomplete/gapped governance framework) read through a different labelling threshold. Per the instruction not to artificially change an assessment where new evidence only strengthens (or, as here, restates) an existing conclusion, S4 remains **Mixed** — this GIRAI evidence is added as reinforcement of the "enabling" sub-dimension already present within that Mixed rating, not as grounds to resolve it toward pure Enabling. This is a documented, non-mechanical judgment call, not a silent override — the researcher may direct otherwise. See [00-Countries/CTY-BRA.md](../00-Countries/CTY-BRA.md) S4 row.
