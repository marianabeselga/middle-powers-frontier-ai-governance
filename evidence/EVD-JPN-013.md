# EVD-JPN-013 — Bletchley participation and Hiroshima Process presentation (Kishida, Nov. 2023)

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-MOFAJP-2023-002; SRC-UKGOV-2023-001 (existing, Bletchley Declaration)
- **Country/case:** CTY-JPN
- **Episode ID:** EP-BLETCHLEY-SEQUENCE-2023
- **Source location:** Japan MFA release, Nov. 2023

## Quotation / paraphrase

Prime Minister Kishida participated online in the first AI Safety Summit, argued international cooperation was necessary for AI governance, and presented the Hiroshima AI Process (a G7-led initiative Japan chaired in 2023) as a potential common foundation for global AI rules, while calling for consultation beyond the G7 including the Global South. Japan endorsed the Bletchley Declaration.

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** P1 (position — international cooperation, existing-instrument entrepreneurship); C4 (diplomatic capability mobilised — G7 presidency position brought into the Summit); M1 (candidate — presenting an existing coalition's output as an entry point for a new one; not itself sufficient for M, see Stage 4 coding table)
- **Evidence strength:** ES2 — Moderate (E1 in substance; source not independently re-fetched — see SRC-MOFAJP-2023-002 Notes)
- **Coding status:** RAW

The Hiroshima AI Process itself is a separately-registered episode (`EP-HIROSHIMA-AI-2024`); only Japan's act of presenting/linking it at Bletchley — an event within this Episode — is coded here, not the Hiroshima Process's own internal coordination or outcomes.
