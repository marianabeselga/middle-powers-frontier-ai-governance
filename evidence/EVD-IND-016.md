# EVD-IND-016 — New Delhi Summit preparation: MeitY-structured Working Group process and international consultations (29 Dec. 2025)

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-PIB-2025-002
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** EP-BLETCHLEY-SEQUENCE-2023
- **Source location:** MeitY/IndiaAI Mission media briefing, 29 Dec. 2025.

## Quotation / paraphrase

MeitY/IndiaAI Mission (chaired by Secretary S. Krishnan) designed the Summit's substantive architecture — three "Sutras" (People, Planet, Progress) operationalised through seven thematic "Chakras"/Working Groups. Directly quoted: "The Working Group themes have been shaped through months of extensive consultations, including public engagement via the MyGov platform, which received over 600 citizen responses, stakeholder consultations involving more than 500 organisations, and multiple national and international brainstorming sessions held in cities such as Oslo, Tokyo, New York, and Paris." Also: "approximately 300 pre-summit events... out of which 57 pre-summits have been held across 25+ countries."

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C4 (institutional/diplomatic resources — an organisational/administrative role, distinct from evidence of coordination; see Researcher notes)
- **Evidence strength:** ES1 — Strong (E1, independently verified in full)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

**Distinguishing India's organisational role from the collective activity it convened (per explicit instruction):** this record establishes that a MeitY-designed framework structured and sequenced an elaborate, internationally-distributed consultative process (500+ organisations, brainstorming sessions in four other countries, ~300 pre-Summit events across 25+ countries). This is stronger and more specific evidence of an *administrative/organisational* role than hosting alone — MeitY did not merely convene a single venue, it designed the thematic structure that channelled a large-scale international consultation. **This is not, by itself, sufficient to code M as established.** The source documents that consultations occurred and that India's government designed the framework for them; it does not document the specific content of any exchange, negotiation, or joint activity between India and named counterparts, nor does it establish that the "500 organisations" or the Oslo/Tokyo/New York/Paris sessions involved India's government directly negotiating or co-producing outputs with specific other actors, as opposed to India's apparatus collecting input submitted by others. See the Episode file's M reassessment for how this is weighed (plausible, not established).
