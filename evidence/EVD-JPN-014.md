# EVD-JPN-014 — Seoul forum-linkage position and interoperability commitment (Kishida, May 2024)

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-MOFAJP-2024-002; SRC-MOFA-2024-001 (existing, Seoul Declaration); SRC-UKGOV-2024-001 (existing, Seoul Statement of Intent)
- **Country/case:** CTY-JPN
- **Episode ID:** EP-BLETCHLEY-SEQUENCE-2023
- **Source location:** Japan MFA release, 21 May 2024

## Quotation / paraphrase

Kishida participated in the Seoul Leaders' Session and explicitly characterised the Hiroshima AI Process and the AI Seoul Summit as "complementary," arguing the Seoul discussions should help operationalise the Hiroshima Code of Conduct. He also linked Japan's AI Safety Institute to development of interoperable safety-evaluation methodologies. Japan was one of the governments represented in both the Seoul Declaration and the Seoul Statement of Intent.

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** P1 (position — forum linkage, interoperability); M1/M3b/M3c (candidate — explicit, stated act of connecting two governance processes; see Stage 4 coding table for the established-vs-plausible assessment); L1/L3 (candidate — agenda-setting and evaluation, via forum linkage)
- **Evidence strength:** ES2 — Moderate (E1 in substance; source not independently re-fetched — see SRC-MOFAJP-2023-002 Notes)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

This is the single most important Japan-specific claim in the Research Note. Distinguish carefully: Kishida's *statement* that the two processes are complementary is a documented diplomatic act (supports M3c at the plausible tier), but it does not by itself show the two processes were *operationally* merged or that a specific joint output resulted — no such output is documented in the supplied material.
