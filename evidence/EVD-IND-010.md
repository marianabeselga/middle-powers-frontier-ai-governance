# EVD-IND-010 — AI Impact Summit 2026 hosting, New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact, and India–Japan AI cooperation

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-PIB-2026-002 (New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact announcement); SRC-MEITY-2026-003 (India–Japan Joint Statement)
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** AI Impact Summit 2026 announcement; India–Japan Joint Statement

## Quotation / paraphrase

"The AI Impact Summit 2026, held in New Delhi on 18–19 February, concluded with the adoption of the New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact... endorsed by 92 countries and international organisations, reflecting a broad-based global consensus on leveraging AI for economic growth and social good." Summit deliverables include the New Delhi Frontier AI Impact Commitments (voluntary commitments by leading AI developers), a Guidance Note on AI Governance, and the Trusted AI Commons (shared repository of open resources).

India–Japan Joint Statement: the two governments "reaffirmed the significance of the Hiroshima AI Process (HAIP), including its international guiding principles and codes of conduct," referenced the Guidance Note on AI Governance prepared by the India AI Impact Summit's Safe & Trusted AI Working Group ("co-chaired by Japan"), and "decided to strengthen coordination... in international fora, including the G20, OECD, GPAI, and the United Nations." They "reaffirmed their commitment to deepen cooperation within the Hiroshima AI Process Friends Group and Partners Community... and promote the implementation of the Hiroshima AI Process Friends Group Action Plan 2026," and agreed to cooperate on AI model evaluation/capability assessment, AI-enabled cybersecurity, and child-safety-by-design.
