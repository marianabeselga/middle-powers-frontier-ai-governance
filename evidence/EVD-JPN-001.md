# EVD-JPN-001 — AI Strategy 2021: national R&D priorities and non-binding strategic framing

## Evidence (from Source)

AI Strategy 2021 (Cabinet Office) sets national priorities for AI R&D and sets out a resilience-oriented framing for AI policy, with a monitoring mechanism via the AI Strategy Execution Council. The strategy is explicitly non-binding — a policy document, not legislation.

## Constructs supported

- **C1 — Technical expertise:** national strategic prioritisation of AI R&D.
- **C2 — Regulatory expertise/capacity:** the strategy itself is non-binding and pre-dates Japan's later binding framework (see [EVD-JPN-002](EVD-JPN-002.md)); contributes background/aspirational context to C2 rather than direct regulatory capacity.

## Notes

Distinguish from [EVD-JPN-002](EVD-JPN-002.md) (Act No. 53 of 2025), which is binding law in force — per Codebook instruction to distinguish laws in force from strategies/policies.
