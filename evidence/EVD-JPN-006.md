# EVD-JPN-006 — Japan AI market size, projected growth, and robotics adoption

## Evidence (from Source)

The US International Trade Administration projects Japan's AI market growing from US$8.9bn to US$27.9bn by 2029; JETRO projects growth from roughly US$780M to US$11.74bn by 2030. The 2026 AI Index Report records US$1.11bn in private AI investment across 56 newly funded companies in Japan (the lowest of the comparator countries checked so far: Canada US$4.28bn/79, India US$4.09bn/108). The OECD Economic Surveys: Japan 2026 documents over 46,000 installed industrial robots and a new AI-Robotics Strategy (March 2026).

## Constructs supported

- **C3 — Market resources:** the combination of substantial and fast-growing projected AI-market size (ITA, JETRO) and an established robotics/industrial-automation base supports **C3 — C-3 (Strong)**, notwithstanding the comparatively low AI Index private-investment figure — treated as one indicator among several rather than determinative on its own, per Codebook §8 (capability levels should not be derived mechanically from a single indicator).

## Notes

The AI Index investment figure (lowest among comparator countries checked) sits in tension with the market-size projections; both are presented rather than one being discarded, consistent with the multi-indicator approach used for Canada's C3.
