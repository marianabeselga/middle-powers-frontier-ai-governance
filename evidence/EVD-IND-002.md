# EVD-IND-002 — IndiaAI Mission: pillars, sovereign foundational models, research/talent pipeline

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-MEITY-2026-001; SRC-INDIAAI-2026-001; SRC-MEITY-2026-002
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** IndiaAI Mission overview and progress-update press releases

## Quotation / paraphrase

IndiaAI Mission launched March 2024 with a budgeted outlay (reported inconsistently across sources — see note below), structured around seven pillars: IndiaAI Compute, Foundation Models, AIKosh (datasets/models/sandbox hub), Application Development Initiative, FutureSkills, Startup Financing, and Safe & Trusted AI. Twelve organisations/consortia (Sarvam AI, Soket AI, Gnani AI, Gan AI, Avataar AI, IIT Bombay Consortium/BharatGen, GenLoop, Zentieq, Intellihealth, Shodh AI, Fractal Analytics, Tech Mahindra Maker's Lab) supported to develop sovereign foundational models; sovereign models from Sarvam AI, BharatGen, and Gnani launched at the IndiaAI Impact Summit 2026, reported to show "strong performance on Indic language benchmarks." A later update reports 20 indigenous model proposals identified (12 LLMs, 8 SLMs). Research/talent pipeline: 500 PhD fellows, 5,000 postgraduates, 8,000 undergraduates targeted; a later update reports 686 fellowships awarded across 178 institutions and 26 lakh individuals completing the "YUVA AI for All" course; 27 IndiaAI Data and AI Labs established via NIELIT (with 543 or 174 additional ITIs/Polytechnics approved across sources); AI Centres of Excellence established across States/UTs (7 in one snapshot, 58 in a later snapshot).

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C1 (Technical expertise)
- **Evidence strength:** ES1 — Strong (E1 primary official; multiple press releases at different reporting dates)
- **Coding status:** RAW
