# EVD-JPN-010 — OECD pilot of the Hiroshima AI Process reporting framework

## Evidence (from Source)

The UK DSIT overview documents an OECD-led pilot of the Hiroshima AI Process reporting framework, covering 10 countries (including Japan and Canada) and roughly 20 organisations, with monitoring against the Hiroshima Code of Conduct.

## Constructs supported

- **C4 — Institutional/diplomatic resources:** Japan's continued participation in operationalising the process it launched (reporting-framework pilot) corroborates sustained institutional/diplomatic engagement, supporting **C4 — C-3 (Strong)** alongside [EVD-JPN-009](EVD-JPN-009.md).
- **S3 — Geopolitical alignments:** multilateral engagement alongside Canada and other OECD members supports **S3 — Enabling**.

## Notes

Per Codebook §11, participation in this reporting-framework pilot is documented here strictly as institutional-capacity/alignment evidence (C4/S3) — it is **not** treated as coordination-mechanism (M) evidence or governance-leverage (GL) evidence, since mere participation is not sufficient evidence of coordination or leverage.
