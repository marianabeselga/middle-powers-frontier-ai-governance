## Quotation / paraphrase

**In force (primary, E1):** CAN-ASC-6.2:2025 — Accessible and Equitable Artificial Intelligence Systems, produced by Accessibility Standards Canada under the Accessible Canada Act, defining disability broadly and setting accessibility principles for AI standards development.

**Withdrawn (primary, E1):** the Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (AIDA), proposed as part of Bill C-27 (2022); "AIDA... was withdrawn and the page archived." The government instead "maintains a risk-based approach through initiatives like the Pan-Canadian AI Strategy and the Directive on Automated Decision-Making for public sector AI use."

## Researcher notes

Researcher's provisional assessment: C2 = C-3 Strong; S4 = Enabling. Researcher observation: "Canada has developed a relatively dense and layered AI governance architecture despite the withdrawal of the proposed [AIDA]... the withdrawal of AIDA should not be interpreted as an absence of domestic AI governance capacity. Rather, Canada's approach remains distributed across multiple institutional and regulatory instruments rather than consolidated in a single horizontal AI Act." 11 of the 14 tracked instruments are recorded as currently In Force (or In Force (Amended)); one (OSFI Guideline E-23) is Awaiting Entry; two (AIDA/Bill C-27 and its Digital Charter Implementation Act vehicle) are Withdrawn — not represented as legislation currently in force anywhere in this record.
