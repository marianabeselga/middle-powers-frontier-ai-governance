# EVD-CAN-002 — AI for All: safety and trust pillar

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-ISED-2026-002 (AI for All)
- **Country/case:** CTY-CAN
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** AI for All, Pillar 1 ("Protecting Canadians and safeguarding our democracy") and "Ensure Canadian AI infrastructure is safe and trustworthy"

## Quotation / paraphrase

"Canada will invest $50 million to expand the capabilities of the Canadian AI Safety Institute to track emerging AI risks, advance technical research, and to conduct transparent evaluations of AI models." Additional key actions: AI transparency/watermarking of AI-generated content; proactive work with frontier AI companies and international partners on AI safety and cybersecurity; a Canada Trusted AI Certification program; renewed funding for the Standards Council of Canada's AI Program ("shape global AI standards and grow a robust AI quality assurance ecosystem," enabling "standards-based AI testing, certification, interoperability, and global market access"); applied AI research partnership with law enforcement/security agencies for fraud, cyber defence, and threat detection.
