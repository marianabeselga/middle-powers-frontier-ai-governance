# EVD-IND-001 — 2018 National Strategy for AI: research structure, ethics, and IP frameworks

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-NITIAAYOG-2018-001
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** —
- **Source location:** National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence #AIforAll (2018), full document

## Quotation / paraphrase

The 2018 strategy identifies five priority sectors (healthcare, agriculture, education, smart cities/infrastructure, smart mobility/transportation) and proposes a two-tiered research structure: Centres of Research Excellence (CORE, "developing better understanding of existing core research and pushing technology frontiers") and International Centers of Transformational AI (ICTAI, "application-based research" with private-sector collaboration); also proposes a "CERN for AI" supranational research agency, a National AI Marketplace (NAIM) for data/models/annotation, an Ethics Council consortium at each CORE built on the FAT (Fairness, Accountability, Transparency) framework, data-protection and sectoral-regulatory framework proposals, and an intellectual-property framework proposal (IP facilitation centres, training for IP authorities/judiciary). The strategy identifies barriers including limited AI research/application expertise, absence of enabling data ecosystems, high resource costs, absent privacy/security regulation, and lack of a collaborative adoption approach.

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C1 (Technical expertise — research-structure proposals); C2 (Regulatory expertise/capacity — ethics/IP/data-protection framework proposals)
