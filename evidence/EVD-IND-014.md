# EVD-IND-014 — India's Paris co-chair role and position (10–11 Feb. 2025)

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-PMINDIA-2025-001; SRC-ELYSEE-2025-001; SRC-ELYSEE-2025-002
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** EP-BLETCHLEY-SEQUENCE-2023
- **Source location:** PM India press release, 11 Feb. 2025.

## Quotation / paraphrase

Prime Minister Modi co-chaired the Paris AI Action Summit alongside President Macron, delivering the opening address at Macron's explicit invitation as co-chair. Modi called for "collective global efforts to establish governance and standards that uphold shared values, address risks and build trust," argued for Global South access to AI and "democratizing technology," and highlighted India's Digital Public Infrastructure and domestic LLM development. India announced it would host the next AI Summit. This adds head-of-government-level detail to the existing Table 3 records (CTY-IND / Paris Co-Chairs' Statement — "working-group co-chair / summit participant"; CTY-IND / Paris Statement — "co-chaired the Summit with France").

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** P1 (position: collective governance, shared values, risk management, Global South access, democratizing AI); C4 (institutional/diplomatic resources — head-of-government co-chair role)
- **Evidence strength:** ES1 — Strong (E1, independently verified in full)
- **Coding status:** RAW
