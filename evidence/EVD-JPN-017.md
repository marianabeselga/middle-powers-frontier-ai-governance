# EVD-JPN-017 — Japan's founding membership, general international-cooperation activity, and joint testing exercise contribution

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-METI-2024-002; SRC-JAISI-2024-001; SRC-UKAISI-2025-001 (existing, independently verified); SRC-ISED-2024-002 (existing, founding membership)
- **Country/case:** CTY-JPN
- **Episode ID:** EP-AISI-NETWORK-2024
- **Source location:** J-AISI establishment announcement; J-AISI international-cooperation activity page; Subsequent Multilateral Joint Testing Exercises report

## Quotation / paraphrase

Japan's AI Safety Institute (J-AISI, established within IPA, 14 Feb. 2024) was a founding member of the Network (21 Nov. 2024) and documents general international exchanges with the US, UK, EU, Canada, Singapore, Australia and Korea. This general activity-mapping is treated as C4/C5 capability context, not as independently establishing M (no specific joint activity with a named partner and an identifiable output is documented in these two sources). **The stronger, independently-verified M-relevant fact:** in the Network's third joint testing exercise, "Australia, Japan and Korea validated Singapore's English dataset annotations," and "Australia, Canada, France, Japan, Kenya, Korea translated datasets into their respective languages and annotated the results" (SRC-UKAISI-2025-001) — specific, documented, differentiated technical work by Japan.

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C4/C5 (capability mobilised); M2/M4 (joint testing exercise contribution)
- **Evidence strength:** ES2 — Moderate for the general cooperation-record claims (not independently re-fetched); ES1 — Strong for the joint testing exercise component (independently verified)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

Consistent with the Bletchley-episode finding for Japan's forum-linkage claim: general "international cooperation/exchange" framing, on its own, does not clear the Codebook §11 Critical Rule (it reads similarly to "general diplomatic contact," explicitly listed as insufficient). **The genuinely established M-relevant fact for Japan is its documented, differentiated participation (dataset validation and translation/annotation) in the third joint testing exercise** — treated as Japan's contribution to the Episode-level "Joint Testing Exercises" configuration, not an independent Japan-specific M finding.
