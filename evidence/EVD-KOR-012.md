# EVD-KOR-012 — Seoul hosting, agenda-setting, and authorship of the Seoul Declaration/Statement of Intent (May 2024)

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-MOFAKR-2024-001; SRC-MOFAKR-2024-002; SRC-MOFA-2024-001 (existing, Seoul Declaration); SRC-UKGOV-2024-001 (existing, Seoul Statement of Intent)
- **Country/case:** CTY-KOR
- **Episode ID:** EP-BLETCHLEY-SEQUENCE-2023
- **Source location:** Korea MOFA releases, May 2024

## Quotation / paraphrase

President Yoon co-chaired the Seoul Leaders' Session and explicitly presented "safety, innovation and inclusivity" as the Summit's three core values, arguing the Summit should become a platform for a new international digital order. Korea's Foreign Ministry characterised the resulting Seoul Declaration and Statement of Intent as providing "a new direction for global AI governance," with Korea committing to work toward "a consistent and interoperable international AI-governance system."

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** P1 (position, host-authored); C4 (diplomatic capability — hosting/agenda-setting, a materially stronger act than mere attendance); M1/M3c (candidate — as host, Korea did not merely sign a text negotiated elsewhere; it structured the agenda and the resulting instruments — this is the strongest host-authorship claim among the four Summits for any of the six cases outside the country that hosted each specific stage); I1/I2 (declarations/agreements — Seoul Declaration, Seoul SOI, both authored under Korea's hosting); L1 (agenda-setting — direct, as host); L7 (institutional design — shaping the SOI's cooperation architecture)
- **Evidence strength:** ES2 — Moderate–Strong (E1 in substance; sources not independently re-fetched — see SRC-MOFAKR-2024-001 Notes)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

Hosting/agenda-setting is treated per Decision Rule 7 (formal membership/access alone is not sufficient) and the Codebook §11 Critical Rule: the question is not whether Korea occupied the host role (documented, undisputed) but whether it *actively used* that role to shape the instruments' content — which Yoon's explicit "three core values" framing and the MOFA's own "new direction" characterisation directly document. This is treated as the strongest M candidate for Korea specifically (see Stage 4 coding table), distinguished from a Table 2 instrument's mere existence (Decision Rule 3).
