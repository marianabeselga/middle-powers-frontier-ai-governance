## Quotation / paraphrase

Two distinct facts, kept separate per the citation-verification finding above. **(1) Research agenda input (not a co-lead role):** the Research Agenda on Risks from Synthetic Content "is supported by... Australia, Canada, France, the European Commission, Japan, Kenya, Korea, Singapore, and the United Kingdom" as a collective Network product; the Network "gathered input from members and external stakeholders" at the November 2024 convening. Canada is one of nine supporting members, not a documented co-lead with Australia specifically. **(2) Joint testing exercise (the stronger, independently-verified fact):** in the Network's third joint testing exercise (Feb.–Jul. 2025), "Australia, Canada, France, Japan, Kenya, Korea translated datasets into their respective languages and annotated the results" (SRC-UKAISI-2025-001) — a specific, documented, differentiated technical contribution by Canada to a completed joint activity led by Singapore and the UK.
