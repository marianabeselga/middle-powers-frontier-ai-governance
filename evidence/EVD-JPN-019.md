## Quotation / paraphrase

Japan was a founding GPAI member, 2023 Lead Chair, hosted the 2022 Tokyo Summit, and established the Tokyo Expert Support Center (December 2023 approval, July 2024 operational at NICT) — a third GPAI expert centre, explicitly designed to support research implementing Hiroshima AI Process principles. Independently confirmed: Japan has named individual expert members in both the Responsible AI Working Group (2 members) and the Innovation & Commercialisation Working Group (three named individuals).
