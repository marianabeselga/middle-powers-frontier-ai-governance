# EVD-IND-015 — India's hosting/convening of the India AI Impact Summit 2026 (16–21 Feb. 2026)

- **Source ID(s):** SRC-PIB-2026-004; SRC-PIB-2026-005; SRC-MEA-2026-001 (existing)
- **Country/case:** CTY-IND
- **Episode ID:** EP-BLETCHLEY-SEQUENCE-2023
- **Source location:** PIB outcomes summary, 11 Mar. 2026; PIB opening-ceremony release, 19 Feb. 2026.

## Quotation / paraphrase

India hosted the Summit (16–21 Feb. 2026, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi) — "the first" global AI summit in the Global South — drawing "more than 100 countries, including 22 Heads of State or Government, and around 10 international organisations." PM Modi inaugurated the Opening Ceremony; Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw positioned India's AI strategy around "democratisation, scale, and sovereignty." The Declaration was endorsed by 92 countries/organisations, with 12 deliverables across 7 working groups and investment announcements exceeding USD 250 billion. This adds convening-scale detail to the existing Table 3 record (CTY-IND / India AI Impact Summit Declaration — "host / endorser").

- **Relevant analytical code(s):** C4 (institutional/diplomatic resources — hosting/convening capacity); P1 (India's own stated strategy: democratisation, scale, sovereignty)
- **Evidence strength:** ES1 — Strong (E1, independently verified)
- **Coding status:** RAW

## Researcher notes

Hosting a large, broadly-endorsed summit is convening-capacity evidence (C4), not by itself evidence of coordination (M) or of governance leverage (GL) attributable to India — per the Codebook §11 Critical Rule and Decision Rule 17, this record does not establish either. Statements by other leaders (Macron, Guterres, Pichai, Amodei) attending the Summit are not India's own position and are not coded here.
