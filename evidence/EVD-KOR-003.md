# EVD-KOR-003 — Governance and enforcement bodies: multi-agency AI institutional architecture

## Evidence (from Source)

Korea's AI governance is distributed across several specialised bodies: MSIT is the central coordinating authority for national AI strategy and the National AI Basic Plan; the National AI Committee, operating under the President, is the AI Basic Act's central cross-administrative coordinating body; the AI Policy Center and AI Safety Institute (Art. 12 of the Act) are dedicated policy/research/safety institutions; the Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC) is the independent data-protection authority enforcing PIPA and issuing AI-privacy guidance; the Korea Communications Commission (KCC) issues generative-AI user-protection guidance; the Korea Internet & Security Agency (KISA) co-publishes AI security guidance with MSIT; and the Korea Institute for Robot Industry Advancement (KIRIA) supports the robotics sector under the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy.

## Constructs supported

- **C4 — Institutional/diplomatic resources:** a distributed but coordinated multi-agency governance architecture, with a Presidentially-chaired central coordinating committee, demonstrates substantial institutional capacity, corroborating [EVD-KOR-006](EVD-KOR-006.md), [EVD-KOR-008](EVD-KOR-008.md), and [EVD-KOR-009](EVD-KOR-009.md).
- **C5 — Evaluation and safety expertise:** the statutory basis for a dedicated AI Safety Institute (Art. 12: risk analysis, safety-evaluation-criteria research, safety-technology and standardisation research, international cooperation) provides direct legal grounding for specialised safety/evaluation capacity, corroborated by the institute's actual 2024 launch documented in [EVD-KOR-008](EVD-KOR-008.md).
