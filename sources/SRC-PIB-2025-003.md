Verified directly, in full. The Safe and Trusted AI Working Group's Conclave (10–11 Dec. 2025, Chennai) was hosted by IIT Madras' Centre for Responsible AI, bringing together government, industry, academia, civil society, and international AI institutions. Key organisational detail: MeitY appointed Prof. Balaraman Ravindran (IIT Madras) as Chair of the Working Group — Prof. Kamakoti (IIT Madras Director) explicitly thanks "MeitY for making my colleague, Prof. B. Ravindran as a co-chair of this very important event." IndiaAI Mission Director Mohammad Y. Safirulla states: "Each of the seven working groups brings together experts from 112 countries and international organisations... The Safe & Trusted AI Working Group's discussions here in Chennai and the upcoming consultations across India will feed directly into actionable, globally relevant outcomes for the Leaders' Declaration." The release explicitly situates the Conclave as building on "global momentum shaped by the UK AI Safety Summit, AI Seoul Summit, France AI Action Summit and the Global AI Summit on Africa" — directly linking this preparatory activity to the Bletchley–Seoul–Paris sequence.
