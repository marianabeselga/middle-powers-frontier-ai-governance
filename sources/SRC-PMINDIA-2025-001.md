## Notes

Verified directly. Confirms Prime Minister Modi co-chaired the Paris AI Action Summit alongside President Macron, delivering the opening address at Macron's invitation as co-chair. Modi called for "collective global efforts to establish governance and standards that uphold shared values, address risks and build trust," advocated for access to AI for the Global South and "democratizing technology," highlighted India's Digital Public Infrastructure and its own LLM development, and announced India would host the next AI Summit. Confirms "the Summit concluded with the adoption of the Leaders' Statement."
