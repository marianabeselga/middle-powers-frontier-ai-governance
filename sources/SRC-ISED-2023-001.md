## Notes

Verified directly. Reports Minister François-Philippe Champagne's participation in the Bletchley AI Safety Summit, where he "led a discussion among governmental leaders from around the globe on risks to safety from frontier AI misuse," and quotes his statement that "it is critical and urgent that we all come together to build public trust around this transformational technology." Confirms the Bletchley Declaration was signed by 28 countries and the EU. States that Canada's approach "aligns with G7 Hiroshima Process principles and the U.S. Executive Order on safe AI development."
