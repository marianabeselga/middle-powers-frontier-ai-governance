## Notes

Reports Singapore as the regional AI leader within ASEAN: US$9bn invested in AI-ready data centres, 7th globally in AI patents per 100,000 inhabitants (2023), and approximately 3.5 AI professionals per 1,000 workers (far above other ASEAN economies), while also noting Singapore remains substantially behind the US and China.
