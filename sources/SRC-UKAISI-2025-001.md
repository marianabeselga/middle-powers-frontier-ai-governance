## Notes

Documents the Network's third joint testing exercise (following exercises in November 2024 and February 2025), split into two strands: leakage of sensitive information/fraud (**led by Singapore's AI Safety Institute**) and cybersecurity (led by the UK AISI). Nine countries plus the EU AI Office participated (Singapore, Japan, Australia, Canada, EU, France, Kenya, Korea, UK). See [SRC-IMDA-2025-003](SRC-IMDA-2025-003.md) for the note's title-attribution issue distinguishing this source from the adjacent bibliography entry.

**Stage 2 cross-reference (2026-09-02):** this Source is reused, not duplicated, for the "Subsequent Multilateral Joint Testing Exercises" instrument in the Coordination Universe Matrix's Table 2/3 — the researcher's Stage 2 mapping independently identified the same URL. This is a distinct instrument from the Table 2 "Network — 2025 technical workshops / evaluation best-practice work" row (sourced to [SRC-UKAISI-2026-001](SRC-UKAISI-2026-001.md)), which cites a different aisi.gov.uk blog post.

**Japan-specific detail, verified 2026-09-02 (re-fetched from the URL above):** "Australia, Japan and Korea validated Singapore's English dataset annotations," and "Australia, Canada, France, Japan, Kenya, Korea translated datasets into their respective languages and annotated the results." This corrects an earlier Coordination Universe Matrix / EP-AISI-NETWORK-2024 characterization that understated Japan's role in this specific (July 2025) exercise as network membership only; Japan is a named participant with documented dataset-validation and translation/annotation activity here, distinct from Japan's non-participation as a lead/participant in the earlier First Multilateral Testing Exercise (2024, SRC-SGAISI-2024-001), which remains unchanged.
