## Notes

**Verification status (updated 2026-09-06):** full text independently fetched and read via the HTML-hosted version of this document (URL above; the original PDF-catalogue link's rendering limitation noted on 2026-09-02 is now superseded by this successful fetch of the same content in HTML form). **Correction, not a silent substitution:** the document's own text does **not** identify Canada and Australia as co-leads of this research agenda. It states the agenda "is supported by, and will be used as a reference in consideration of work planning and prioritization by, Australia, Canada, France, the European Commission, Japan, Kenya, Korea, Singapore, and the United Kingdom" — i.e., it is presented as a collective Network-wide product supported by nine members generally, with no country singled out as a lead or co-lead. It further states that "[a]t the inaugural Network convening held in November 2024, the Network gathered input from members and external stakeholders to inform the further elaboration of the research agenda" — again collective, not attributed to any specific pair of countries. **The "Canada and Australia co-lead" characterisation in Canada's Research Note and in `EVD-CAN-010`'s researcher-observation field is therefore not supported by this primary source and should not be treated as verified evidence of a Canada–Australia bilateral coordination mechanism.** This document remains valid evidence of an Episode-level, Network-wide institutional instrument (I3 — a non-binding research-priorities guidance document) and of Canada's institutional access/participation, but does not by itself establish country-specific M for Canada.
