## Notes

Tracker table lists 13 instruments. Unlike Japan and Korea, **none of the 13 tracked instruments is a comprehensive, horizontal, binding AI statute** — Singapore's AI-specific governance layer consists entirely of non-binding frameworks/guidelines (Updated Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI, In Force 2026; AI Verify, In Force 2022; PDPC/MAS guidance, In Force 2018–2024) and policies (NAIS 2.0, 2023; AI Singapore, 2017), plus bills (OSRA Bill, Under Review 2025; Workplace Fairness Bill, Adopted 2025). The only tracked instrument with Type="Act" and Status="In Force" is the Elections (Integrity of Online Advertising) (Amendment) Act 2024, which is an elections-integrity statute with AI/deepfake-adjacent provisions, not a general AI law. The Research Note's narrative text separately references the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) as a further binding statute governing AI-related personal-data processing, though PDPA is not itself one of the 13 tracked instruments in this table. **Type/Status labelling note:** the "Workplace Fairness Bill" entry is typed "Bill" with status "Adopted" and its own title parenthetical states the legislation "passed in Parliament" — this internal inconsistency (Bill-type label vs. an enacted-legislation description) is preserved as a documentation flag, not resolved by relabelling. See [EVD-SGP-002](../02-Evidence/EVD-SGP-002.md) for the full C2/S4 treatment of this distinction.
