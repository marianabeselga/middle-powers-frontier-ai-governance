Verified directly, in full. MeitY/IndiaAI Mission briefed media on the Summit's structure (three "Sutras" — People, Planet, Progress — operationalised through seven thematic "Chakras"/Working Groups) and progress. Secretary S. Krishnan's remarks frame the Summit's goal as ensuring broad international access to AI infrastructure (compute, models, data). Confirms directly: "The Working Group themes have been shaped through months of extensive consultations, including public engagement via the MyGov platform, which received over 600 citizen responses, stakeholder consultations involving more than 500 organisations, and multiple national and international brainstorming sessions held in cities such as Oslo, Tokyo, New York, and Paris." Also confirms "approximately 300 pre-summit events... out of which 57 pre-summits have been held across 25+ countries."
