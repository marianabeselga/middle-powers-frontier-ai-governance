## Notes

Tracker table lists 22 instruments with an explicit Type/Status/Year breakdown — the most granular regulatory-landscape table encountered so far across the six cases. Only three tracked instruments are binding Acts currently In Force: the AI Basic Act (2025, see [SRC-LAWGO-2025-001](SRC-LAWGO-2025-001.md)), the amended Personal Information Protection Act (2023), and the Act on the Development and Supply of Intelligent Robots (2008, Amended). All AI-specific horizontal legislative attempts prior to the AI Basic Act were withdrawn or remain merely proposed: AI Responsibility and Regulation Bill (Withdrawn, 2023), Artificial Intelligence Responsibility Act (Withdrawn, 2023), AI Industry Promotion and Trust-Building Bill (Proposed, 2022), Act on Algorithms and Artificial Intelligence (Withdrawn, 2021), AI Promotion and Trust-Building Bill (Withdrawn, 2021), Bill on Artificial Intelligence (Withdrawn, 2021), Basic Act on AI Technology (Withdrawn, 2020). The remaining instruments are policies (National AI Basic Plan 2026–2028, Digital Strategy 2022, Digital Bill of Rights 2023, National Strategy for AI 2019 — see [SRC-MSIT-2019-001](SRC-MSIT-2019-001.md)) or non-binding guidelines/notices (Generative AI Service User Protection Guideline, AI Security Guide, various PIPC guidance documents, National AI Ethics Standards). What Regulations.ai reports is preserved as distinct from what the underlying legal texts would independently establish; primary retrieval remains available selectively where a future claim depends on a specific instrument's precise legal content, scope, obligation, implementation, or effect (same rule as applied to [SRC-REGAI-2026-001](SRC-REGAI-2026-001.md) for CTY-BRA).
