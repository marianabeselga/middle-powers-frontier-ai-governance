## Notes

Reports Singapore's trade/energy external exposure (goods exports 107.9% of GDP, goods imports 78.7% of GDP in 2025; >90% of electricity from imported natural gas; net oil/gas imports ~7% of GDP) alongside substantial financial buffers (current-account surplus 16.7% of GDP; net international investment position 179.1% of GDP; FX reserves ~US$409bn / 68% of GDP); IMF assesses Singapore's external position as "substantially stronger" than implied by medium-term fundamentals.
