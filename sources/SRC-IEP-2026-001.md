## Notes

**India classification note (2026-08-31):** unlike Brazil and Canada, which this report classifies as "middle powers," the India evidence cited in the India Research Note characterises India as a **"great power"** ("India is the sole great power nation with strong projected economic growth... has surpassed China as the world's most populous nation and is projected to become the third-largest economy before 2030"). This is not a contradiction — the same report applies different tier classifications to different countries — but it is flagged for researcher awareness given the project's "middle powers" framing; it does not block Stage 1 C4/S3 coding, which proceeds on India's documented institutional/diplomatic capabilities regardless of tier label. See [EVD-IND-008](../02-Evidence/EVD-IND-008.md) and the CTY-IND processing report.

**Japan classification note (2026-08-31):** the Japan Research Note likewise cites this report characterising Japan as part of "the greater power group" (citing Japan's 3rd-highest nominal GDP). As with India, this "great power"/"middle power" tier label is the IEP's own classification and is **not reinterpreted or altered** — it is a separate theoretical/case-selection matter for the researcher, not a Stage 1 coding issue, and does not block Japan's C4/S3 coding on documented institutional/diplomatic capabilities. See [EVD-JPN-008](../02-Evidence/EVD-JPN-008.md).

**Korea classification note (2026-08-31):** unlike India and Japan, the Korea Research Note's evidence from this report characterises South Korea explicitly as a **rising middle power** — "South Korea's trajectory is particularly notable, as it has surged past established middle powers like Australia and Canada to occupy a high-bandwidth, high-dependence position that rivals the top European states" — not as part of the "great power"/"greater power" group. This is consistent with, not in tension with, the project's "AI middle power" framing; no reinterpretation issue arises here. See [EVD-KOR-006](../02-Evidence/EVD-KOR-006.md).

**Singapore classification note (2026-09-01):** the Singapore Research Note's evidence from this report characterises Singapore as an explicitly **distinct fourth category** — "Singapore emerging power (different from others), not a middle or major power — increasing bilateral power in other ASEAN countries, 1991–2023." This is neither the "great power" label applied to India/Japan, nor the "(rising) middle power" label applied to Brazil/Canada/Korea — it is the IEP's own "emerging power" category. As with the other five cases, this classification is **not reinterpreted or altered**; it is flagged for researcher awareness as a fourth distinct tier label appearing across the six cases, a case-selection/theoretical matter for the researcher rather than a Stage 1 coding issue. See [EVD-SGP-006](../02-Evidence/EVD-SGP-006.md).
