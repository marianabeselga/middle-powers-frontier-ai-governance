## Notes

Documents the National AI Computing Center / "AI Highway" initiative, the subsequently announced plan to secure more than 260,000 NVIDIA GPUs (~50,000 public sector; 200,000+ private sector, including Samsung, SK Group, Hyundai Motor Group, and NAVER) — an **announced target**, not yet operational capacity — and the domestic AI-semiconductor-use target for the Center (originally 50% by 2030, subsequently loosened toward a more flexible public-private adoption model).
