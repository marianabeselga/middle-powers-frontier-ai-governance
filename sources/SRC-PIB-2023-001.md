## Notes

Verified directly. Minister of State Rajeev Chandrasekhar addressed the Bletchley AI Safety Summit's inaugural plenary, framing India's approach around safety, trust, and accountability. Quotes PM Modi's stated general view (via Chandrasekhar) that "the future of technology... should be driven by a coalition of nations rather than just one or two countries," and that "the institutional framework should be less episodic and much more sustained with strategic clarity." Additional quotes: "We want AI and tech to represent goodness, safety and trust"; "AI should be utilized only for the good, only for the progress, and prosperity of all our citizens across all countries." This is a normative **position statement** advocating multilateral governance — it is not, by itself, documentation of an actual coalition-formation process or interaction (see EVD-IND-013 for the coding distinction).
