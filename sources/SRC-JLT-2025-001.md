## Notes

**Binding law in force**, not a bill, strategy, or soft-law framework. Establishes the AI Strategic Headquarters (chaired by the Prime Minister) and requires an AI Basic Plan. Characterized in the Research Note as "light-touch" relative to the EU AI Act: no mandatory risk classification, conformity assessment, or pre-market approval regime; relies on guidance, coordination, and periodic review. This is distinct in legal status from Brazil's pending PL 2338/2023, Canada's withdrawn AIDA, and India's non-binding AI governance guidelines — see [CTY-JPN.md](../00-Countries/CTY-JPN.md) C2/S4 justifications for the comparative framing.
