## Closed items (no further researcher decision required)

- **S3/S4 in Inbox Note 1:** listed as possible constructs without a rating or rationale. Classified as **Not coded — insufficient basis in this note**, a closed, permanent classification, not an open question. The original references remain untouched in the raw Inbox note. The S3 = Enabling and S4 = Mixed rows above were assessed using other, sufficient evidence (Notes 5 and 4 respectively), not inferred from Note 1.
- **GPAI / ISO-IEC JTC 1/SC 42 / Bletchley Declaration (C4):** researcher-confirmed observations; documentary sourcing pending Stage 2 — a defined future step, not an open item. See [EVD-BRA-006](../02-Evidence/EVD-BRA-006.md).
- **Rmax vintage difference (S1):** resolved as a publication-date vintage difference, not a contradiction; both figures retained, June 2026 TOP500 figure used as current. See [EVD-BRA-008](../02-Evidence/EVD-BRA-008.md).
- **Attribution/date discrepancies in the 2026-08-31 material:** the IBGE-attributed internet-use statistic resolves to a U.S. Department of Commerce (trade.gov) URL rather than an IBGE-hosted one — recorded as reported, not corrected (see [SRC-TRADE-2025-001](../01-Sources/SRC-TRADE-2025-001.md)); the GIRAI source's bibliographic Date (2025) and its own Evidence text ("2026 GIRAI") disagree — both retained (see [SRC-GIRAI-2025-001](../01-Sources/SRC-GIRAI-2025-001.md)).
- **Unsourced Pix payment-system statistic:** cited in the Researcher Observation without an accompanying Source entry in this note; preserved as observation text, not extracted into formal Evidence. See [EVD-BRA-011](../02-Evidence/EVD-BRA-011.md).
- **S4 assessment retained as Mixed despite the new note's own "Enabling" label (2026-08-31):** a considered judgment, not a mechanical pick — see the S4 row's Confidence/flag column and [EVD-BRA-012](../02-Evidence/EVD-BRA-012.md) for full reasoning. Open to researcher override.
- **C1, C4, and C2/S4 calibration — confirmed consistent with the Codebook by researcher decision, 2026-09-02, no revision.** A controlled Stage 1 consistency review (cross-checking Brazil's C1, C4, and C2/S4 ratings against Canada, India, and Singapore, and against the Codebook's own text rather than cross-case symmetry) found: **C1** (Moderate) rests on a primary-source (E1/ES1) self-admission in the PBIA itself of a specific capacity constraint (professional shortage, brain drain, foreign-patent dependence) — a genuine evidentiary basis not present in comparably-rated cases, not an inconsistency (Codebook §18/§22 Rule 11: primary evidence should not be displaced by index evidence alone). **C4** (Strong) rests entirely on formally-sourced Evidence (G20 Rio Declaration, BRICS GGAI Declaration, IEP — see [EVD-BRA-006](../02-Evidence/EVD-BRA-006.md)); the separately-flagged GPAI/ISO/Bletchley material is correctly not treated as equivalent to formal Evidence and does not support the rating (Codebook §20/§25). **C2/S4 calibration** relative to Canada/India/Singapore is grounded in each case's own evidence of institutional/administrative/enforcement-capacity maturity (Codebook §8/§16), not in the presence or absence of a single binding AI statute — a legitimate basis for differentiated ratings, not an inconsistency. No assessment was changed as a result of this review. See `07-Audit-Trail/Coding-Decisions-Log.md` for the full consistency-review record.

No item above is classified as "Unresolved — researcher decision required." **Brazil Stage 1 is closed.**
