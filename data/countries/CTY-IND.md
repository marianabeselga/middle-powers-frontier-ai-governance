## Closed items (no further researcher decision required)

- **Budget-figure discrepancy (IndiaAI Mission outlay):** reported as ₹10,371 crore, ₹10,372 crore, and "₹10,300+ crore" across three press releases — likely the same underlying allocation; all three retained per Codebook §22 Decision Rule 12, not reconciled to a single figure. Does not affect the C1/S1 assessments. See [EVD-IND-002](../02-Evidence/EVD-IND-002.md).
- **GPU/compute figures reported at successive snapshots** (e.g. 38,231 GPUs/14 providers vs. a later 15-provider/237-project/93-lakh-GPU-hour update): treated as successive progress updates, not merged or contradictory, consistent with the CTY-BRA vintage-difference precedent. See [EVD-IND-002](../02-Evidence/EVD-IND-002.md), [EVD-IND-003](../02-Evidence/EVD-IND-003.md).
- **UNCTAD ranking attribution:** the raw note's formatting placed India's UNCTAD figures under a header nominally assigned to a different (PIB) source; attributed to UNCTAD based on exact format match to the CTY-BRA/CTY-CAN UNCTAD entries — a transparent, content-based attribution, not a fabrication. See [SRC-UNCTAD-2025-001](../01-Sources/SRC-UNCTAD-2025-001.md) and [EVD-IND-011](../02-Evidence/EVD-IND-011.md).
- **Duplicate/erroneous URLs and source labels — now corrected by the researcher (2026-08-31):** (a) the "India AI Governance Guidelines" source originally shared its URL with the IndiaAI Safety Institute announcement; the researcher added a new bibliographic entry ("Source #7") with the document's genuine URL, title, date, and MeitY attribution — [SRC-PIB-2026-001](../01-Sources/SRC-PIB-2026-001.md) now uses the corrected citation, and the old erroneous entry remains in the raw note as an orphaned, unreferenced stub (preserved, not deleted). (b) The India–Japan Joint Statement's original citation ([SRC-MEITY-2026-003](../01-Sources/SRC-MEITY-2026-003.md)) is now known to have actually belonged to a *different* document (the New Delhi Declaration announcement); the researcher supplied the statement's genuinely correct MEA citation, and the New Delhi Declaration announcement ([SRC-PIB-2026-002](../01-Sources/SRC-PIB-2026-002.md)) now has its own correct PIB URL — previously it had none. None of these corrections changed any quoted evidence content or the C4/S3 assessment.
- **"New Delhi Declaration" naming distinction:** the GPAI New Delhi Declaration ([EVD-IND-007](../02-Evidence/EVD-IND-007.md)) and the New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact from the February 2026 AI Impact Summit ([EVD-IND-010](../02-Evidence/EVD-IND-010.md)) are two different documents sharing a similar name — kept explicitly distinguished throughout.
- **India's "great power" classification (IEP) — confirmed unchanged, 2026-08-31:** the IEP source content is untouched by this revision. Per explicit researcher instruction, this classification is **not** reinterpreted, altered, or treated as an error to reconcile — it is accurately represented as the IEP's own taxonomy. The project's "AI middle power" concept is analytically distinct from the IEP's power-tier classification; any tension between the two is a separate theoretical/case-selection question for the researcher, not a Stage 1 coding issue. Does not affect the C4/S3 assessment, which rests on India's documented capabilities regardless of power-tier label. See [EVD-IND-008](../02-Evidence/EVD-IND-008.md).

## Notes

- Distinguish capability existence from mobilisation (mobilisation is coded at episode level, not here).
- Where evidence is insufficient or conflicting, mark as uncertain — do not infer.
- No aggregate C or S score is calculated, per Codebook §20.
- Institutional/diplomatic membership, convening, and participation (C4) are coded strictly as capability/institutional-access evidence per Codebook §8; they are not treated as evidence of coordination (M) or governance leverage (GL) per the Codebook §11 Critical rule.
- The strategy-vs-enacted-instrument and soft-law-vs-binding-law distinctions (Codebook-consistent) are preserved throughout: the 2018 National Strategy and the 2025/2026 Governance Guidelines are policy/strategy documents, not legislation; only the IT Rules 2021 among the tracked instruments is a binding regulation currently in force, and the DPDP Act 2023 is not yet in force (Awaiting Entry).
