## Closed items (no further researcher decision required)

- **Law-in-force distinction (Act No. 53 of 2025):** Japan's AI Promotion Act is a genuinely binding statute already in force — unlike Brazil's pending PL 2338/2023, Canada's withdrawn AIDA, or India's non-binding governance guidelines. This distinction is preserved explicitly in the C2/S4 justifications above rather than treated as equivalent to the other four cases' non-binding instruments. See [EVD-JPN-002](../02-Evidence/EVD-JPN-002.md).
- **C2 header/rationale inconsistency (Research Note, Note 1):** the note's summary header referenced "Strong" for C2, but its detailed rationale explicitly concludes Japan's C2 should be assessed as **Moderate** (in contrast with Canada's Strong), citing the Act's light-touch, non-prescriptive design. Resolved by following the fuller rationale text, consistent with how header-vs-rationale mismatches were resolved in prior countries' processing — this is treated as one note's own internal inconsistency, not a genuine cross-note conflict requiring an "Unresolved" flag. See [EVD-JPN-002](../02-Evidence/EVD-JPN-002.md), [EVD-JPN-003](../02-Evidence/EVD-JPN-003.md).
- **Shared URL between AI Business Operator Guidelines and J-AISI citations:** the Research Note's bibliography lists two distinctly titled entries with the identical URL (a dated METI advisory-council report page). Registered as one Source ([SRC-METI-2026-001](../01-Sources/SRC-METI-2026-001.md)) supporting two Evidence records ([EVD-JPN-003](../02-Evidence/EVD-JPN-003.md), [EVD-JPN-004](../02-Evidence/EVD-JPN-004.md)) per ID Register Rule 7, since Japanese government advisory-council report pages routinely bundle multiple agenda items — treated as a plausible shared-document situation, not flagged as an error.
- **Date-format ambiguity (US ITA source):** cited date "31/08/2026" read as DD/MM/YYYY (31 August 2026), since no month numbered above 12 exists, ruling out MM/DD/YYYY. See [SRC-ITA-2026-001](../01-Sources/SRC-ITA-2026-001.md).
- **Japan's "great power" classification (IEP) — not reinterpreted:** as with India, the IEP's "The Great Fragmentation" report groups Japan among the "greater power group" (citing Japan's 3rd-highest nominal GDP). Per the same policy established for India, this classification is **not** reinterpreted, altered, or treated as an error — it is the IEP's own taxonomy, accurately represented, and analytically distinct from the project's "AI middle power" concept. Does not affect the C4/S3 assessment, which rests on Japan's documented capabilities regardless of power-tier label. See [EVD-JPN-008](../02-Evidence/EVD-JPN-008.md).
- **AI Index 2026 table:** Japan's figures (US$1.11bn / 56 companies — the lowest among comparator countries checked so far: Canada US$4.28bn/79, India US$4.09bn/108) are now recorded, matching the figures already flagged as pending in [SRC-AIINDEX-2026-001](../01-Sources/SRC-AIINDEX-2026-001.md). Singapore/South Korea/Brazil figures in the same table remain unrecorded, flagged for reuse when those profiles are processed.
- **Candidate Stage 2 material identified, not acted on:** the G7 Leaders' Statement on the Hiroshima Process and the OECD pilot of the Hiroshima AI Process reporting framework are directly relevant to the Hiroshima AI Process entry already seeded in [03-Episodes/Coordination-Universe-Matrix.md](../03-Episodes/Coordination-Universe-Matrix.md); flagged for future Stage 2 addition, not touched in this Stage 1 task. See [EVD-JPN-009](../02-Evidence/EVD-JPN-009.md), [EVD-JPN-010](../02-Evidence/EVD-JPN-010.md).
